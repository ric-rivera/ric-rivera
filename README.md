### 🚀 Featured Work

* [CLOUS: Cross-Linguistic Orthographic Uniqueness Score](https://github.com/ric-rivera/CLOUS-tokenization-metrics)  
  My first independent research project, sparked by a simple question: “Which country name is the most unique?”

  I developed a character-frequency-based interpretation of orthographic uniqueness, built a multilingual country-name benchmark, and investigated its relationship to tokenization fragmentation across six tokenizers and eleven languages.

  The project includes a Python evaluation pipeline, statistical comparisons, a research manuscript, and an interactive Hugging Face application. It demonstrates end-to-end analytical work—from defining a question and assembling data to evaluating results and communicating limitations.
